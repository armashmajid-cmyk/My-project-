The industry challenges

We focus on the problems that quietly reduce factory profit.

Paper and board manufacturers manage complex processes, costly resources and demanding delivery schedules. Disconnected information makes those challenges harder to control.

♧

Raw material volatility

Uncertain supplier prices, material shortages and weak purchasing visibility can increase production costs.

ϟ

High energy consumption

Limited machine-level energy data makes it difficult to identify abnormal usage and efficiency opportunities.

⚙

Unexpected downtime

Unplanned equipment failures interrupt production, delay orders and increase maintenance pressure.

◎

Quality defects and waste

Late defect detection can lead to rejected batches, rework, material waste and customer complaints.

▤

Inventory blind spots

Disconnected stock records can cause overstocking, shortages, misplaced goods and slow dispatch.

◷

Planning and cost uncertainty

When orders, capacity and cost data are separated, managers struggle to plan accurately and protect margins.
